Commentary

Liquidity takes centre stage.

The key theme for October is liquidity. October produced just 10 recorded sales across Devonport, Belmont, Bayswater, Narrow Neck and Stanley Point.

That takes the Peninsula to 162 sales for the first ten months of 2026, which is exactly the same number recorded through October 2024. Across the latest 12 months, 208 properties have sold compared with 280 in the preceding 12 month period, a reduction of 25.7%.

The average sale price across the Peninsula was approximately $1.646m, down 7.1% from approximately $1.771m previously.

With transaction numbers this low, individual higher or lower value sales can materially influence average prices, so I think the price figures need to be considered alongside the much more significant reduction in sales activity.

For a sustained improvement in market conditions, I would want to see sales volumes begin recovering consistently rather than relying solely on movements in average prices.

Perspective

October perspective.

October reinforces the theme we have been watching throughout much of 2026. The market is functioning, but turnover remains heavily constrained.

Good homes continue to sell when price, presentation and positioning align with buyer expectations.

For sellers, the current environment places greater importance on accurate pricing and creating genuine competition rather than relying on general market momentum.

For buyers, lower transaction volumes mean patience remains important. Quality opportunities exist, but the best properties can still attract competition.
