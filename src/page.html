Most agents can show you a map of the Peninsula. Ben can tell you who lives on it.

Ben Potter brings a rare blend of energy, experience and a fresh outlook to real estate on Auckland's North Shore. Thirty-eight years living across Devonport, Belmont and Bayswater have given him the kind of knowledge that can't be learned from a spreadsheet: which streets are tightly held, which buyers are waiting, and what a home needs to look like before they walk through the door.

His drive to deliver premium results, paired with a genuine passion for people and property, is why locals trust him with the peninsula's most sought-after homes. Ben doesn't cover these suburbs. He lives in them.
